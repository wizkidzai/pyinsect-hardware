🔒 Privacy policy

How PyInsect treats your information. Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

PyInsect is made for kids. We count how many people visit and which pages they open, so we can make it better. We use GoatCounter for this. It uses no cookies, does not save your IP address, and we never use any of this for ads.

📊 What we count

We use GoatCounter, a small, privacy-friendly visitor counter, to see how many people use PyInsect. It keeps only totals:

  • which pages or screens are opened, and how many times
  • the country people are in (never a street or exact spot)
  • the kind of browser and device (for example "Chrome on Android"), and screen size
  • which website sent people here

GoatCounter uses no cookies and does not save your IP address. It does not follow you from site to site.

🚫 What we never collect

  • Names, emails, accounts or passwords (there are none)
  • Your IP address, or cookies of any kind
  • Your exact location, street or home address
  • Photos, camera video, or anything you type or choose in the app. Only the page's address is sent, never the part after a "?".
  • Anything used to show you ads. We do not use this data for ads or share it for advertising.

💾 Your data in the app

Photos you take stay on your own computer or phone. They are never uploaded by this app.

⚖️ Children's privacy

We do not ask for names, emails or accounts, and we do not try to find out who anyone is. We only look at totals. If a parent or guardian is worried, they can block GoatCounter in their browser (for example with a content blocker), and the app still works. A grown-up can also ask us to look into any question by contacting us below.

👀 Who can see the numbers

Only the PyInsect team and a grown-up who looks after the project can see the totals, through a private link.

💬 Questions

Ask a grown-up to contact the Wizkidz AI team through github.com/wizkidzai.
